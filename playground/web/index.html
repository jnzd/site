Lex playground

Lex turns legal provisions into rules a computer can enforce. Write Lex on the left and compile it to metric first-order temporal logic (MFOTL), and browse it as Lexdoc. Everything runs in your browser: the Lex compiler, and Z3 for checking refinements (.rex files), which loads the first time one needs it (about 9 MB).
